Connected surfaces

Different jobs. One planning system.

ChronoSpark separates capture, scheduling, guidance, evidence review, and reflection so one screen does not pretend to do everything.

01 / ORIENT

Nexus

Review current planning context and the next decision without invented personal precision.

02 / CREATE

Creator

Draft an action, review the preview, confirm it, and keep a reversible receipt.

03 / PLACE

Timeline

See scheduled action as a plan, not as a false deadline or automatic commitment.

04 / PLAN

Smart Planner

Work through a stated situation with bounded guidance and clear next steps.

05 / EXAMINE

SI Console

Inspect evidence, assumptions, limits, and recommendations before acting.

06 / REFLECT

Trajectory + Progression

Compare scenarios and outcomes without presenting a forecast as personal truth.

Trust contract

What the system can say today.

A future-facing product earns trust by distinguishing shipped capability, disabled capability, and long-term direction.

Visible reasoning

Evidence, assumptions, confidence, and tradeoffs remain inspectable where the product has enough information.

User-controlled action

Creator confirms before saving, and guidance does not silently rewrite a person's plan.

No whole-person claim

ChronoSpark is not publicly represented as synthetic emotional intelligence or a verified whole-person companion.
