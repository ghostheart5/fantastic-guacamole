System map

One coherent human loop.

ChronoSpark organizes the work of understanding, choosing, acting, and learning. Each surface has a bounded responsibility.

01 / Nexus

Orient before acting.

Nexus brings current planning signals into one operating view and identifies where the next decision belongs.

  • Does not invent a personal score when evidence is thin.
  • Distinguishes missing signals from negative signals.
  • Hands work to Creator, Timeline, or a guidance surface.
02 / Creator

Create with confirmation.

Creator turns a deliberate input into a preview, confirmation, receipt, and reversible result.

  • Nothing is saved from the draft alone.
  • The user reviews the exact object before confirmation.
  • Receipts and undo preserve control after the save.
03 / Timeline

Place action in time.

Timeline projects scheduled work without treating every planned time as a missed deadline.

  • Scheduled time and due time remain different facts.
  • Planned action stays editable and reviewable.
  • Outcomes can return to the reflection loop.
04 / Smart Planner

Work through the immediate situation.

Smart Planner uses the information provided for a bounded planning response and a concrete next move.

  • Guidance is not represented as diagnosis or certainty.
  • Follow-up language stays focused on the current planning problem.
  • External generative explanation remains release-disabled.
05 / SI Console

Examine evidence and limits.

SI Console is a read-only evidence and recommendation surface for deeper investigation.

  • Facts, calculations, inferences, and missing data stay distinct.
  • Unsupported conclusions can be refused.
  • Recommendations remain subject to user judgment.
06 / Trajectory

Compare scenarios, not destinies.

Trajectory exposes assumptions and uncertainty so a scenario can be examined instead of mistaken for a personal prediction.

  • Scenario inputs and coefficients remain visible.
  • Receipts support later comparison with observed outcomes.
  • Precision is withheld when evidence cannot support it.
07 / Progression

Reflect without manufacturing identity.

Progression records evidence-backed movement and supports deliberate adjustment over time.

  • Fresh users are not assigned an evidence-free archetype.
  • Completion, deferral, and reflection can inform later planning.
  • The person can correct what the system got wrong.
