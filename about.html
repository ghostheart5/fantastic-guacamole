About ChronoSpark

Understand your day. Choose a next step that fits.

ChronoSpark brings goals, tasks, daily rhythms and notes into a planning experience that keeps the reasoning visible and the choice yours.

Made for real life

Some days offer a focused hour. Others leave five minutes before school pickup. Use Smart Planner to work through the situation you describe, review relevant planning evidence in SI Console, and confirm the actions you want to save.

Guidance you can question

See what a suggestion is based on, what information is missing and what choices remain. Scenarios are possibilities, not predictions you have to accept. Your notes, chosen context and recorded actions support the plan without defining who you are.

No ads. Ever.

ChronoSpark will never show advertisements. No user will have to watch an ad for credits, features or access. The same promise applies to every plan; there is no paid ad-removal tier.

Clear about availability

The current release provides on-device planning guidance in English. Subscriptions, credit purchases and external AI actions are limited to eligible private testing accounts. Available paid actions show their costs before confirmation. Cloud sync, restore, analytics and crash reporting remain disabled.

Planning guidance does not replace professional or emergency support. Review the privacy policy for account, voice and data-handling details.
