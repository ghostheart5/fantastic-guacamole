================================================================================
6) HIGHEST-RISK CONFUSING TERMS
================================================================================
1. Smart Coach vs Smart Planner
- Risk: Users may not know if these are separate AI products or the same surface.

2. Progression vs Ascension
- Risk: Two names for one momentum/progress concept split identity and weaken UX coherence.

3. Future Vector vs Trajectory/Forecast naming mix
- Risk: Predictive features appear fragmented rather than one clear destination.

4. Nexus vs Home
- Risk: Home navigation expectations clash with branded naming when not explicitly mapped.

5. Mission Zero / Activation residue
- Risk: Legacy setup metaphors feel like a different product language and may confuse onboarding expectations.

6. Tactical / Command phrasing
- Risk: Tone drifts from planner product to militarized/console semantics; can reduce clarity and trust.
