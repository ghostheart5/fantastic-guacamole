SECTION 1
EXECUTIVE ANIMATION SUMMARY
============================================================

Current animation maturity:
- Moderate to strong.
- The app already uses multiple Flutter motion systems (AnimationController, Animated* widgets, CustomPainter, Lottie overlays).

Strongest motion patterns:
- Short contextual overlays (focus start, level-up, success feedback).
- Micro-interactions in input/action controls (opacity and container transitions).
- Consistent neon visual language with pulse/glow motifs.

Weakest motion patterns:
- Limited global reduced-motion coverage across all animation surfaces.
- Multiple simultaneous ambient loops in onboarding-style surfaces can become visually busy.
- Motion behavior consistency varies by feature area.

Productivity impact:
- Motion usually helps orientation and feedback.
- In a few surfaces, continuous pulse/glow/ambient effects can compete with focus if overexposed.

Premium vs excessive:
- Mostly premium and intentional.
- Borderline excessive in certain ambient/pulse combinations.

Verdict:
EXPAND CAREFULLY

============================================================

- repeated AnimatedBuilder usage in dense widget trees
- potential battery cost from always-on ambient effects

Lower risk findings:

Jank risk level:
- medium

Risk controls recommended:

SECTION 11
MOTION DESIGN SYSTEM RECOMMENDATION
============================================================

Recommended rules (design-only, no implementation):

Duration ranges:
- micro feedback: 100-180ms
- control/state transitions: 140-260ms
- modal emphasis: 180-320ms
- ambient loops: 3-12s with low amplitude

Easing:
- default: easeOutCubic for entry/feedback
- continuity: easeInOut for subtle ambient drift
- avoid bouncy easings in productivity-critical flows

When to animate:
- confirmation
- status transition
- focus direction
- loading in progress

When not to animate:
- dense text reading regions
- repeated daily tasks where speed is priority
- critical decision points requiring calm cognition

Success motion:
- short, clean, one-shot, no loop

Error motion:
- minimal attention cue; avoid aggressive shake/flashing

Loading motion:
- calm loop + clear text context + timeout fallbacks

Navigation motion:
- keep route transitions subtle and consistent
