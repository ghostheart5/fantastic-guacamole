# Axiomara release requirements: policy, engineering and optional advice

Owner-authorized correction, September 26, 2026 UTC. Current product scope is planning, reflection and decision support, with optional self-reported energy/emotional context and optional generative AI. No diagnosis, treatment, clinical-effectiveness or medical-device claim is approved by this record.

## Origin and correction

Git blame traces the two mandatory signed qualified-review rows in EXTERNAL_GATES.md to commit 119b4f5fb on August 29. They were internal project controls, not evidence of a Google mandate. PR 136's initial public-profile preparation then encoded a blanket requirement for independent legal and mental-health assessments. That overstated what the checked policies require for the current product scope.

The owner requested that unsupported requirements be corrected. Outside privacy/legal and mental-health professional signatures are now optional advice for this scope, unless a concrete applicable law, regulated claim or policy is identified that requires them. No historical approval is fabricated. Privacy/disclosure and AI-safety validation remain factual release obligations, performed and documented by the release owner and technical team. The new source names describe validation rather than professional certification; both remain false while final evidence is incomplete.

## What is actually required

| Lane | Requirement and evidence | Who can do the work |
|---|---|---|
| Google declarations and privacy | Accurate health/feature classification, privacy policy, Data safety, necessary disclosures/consent and account/data deletion; reconcile actual distributed behavior and provider handling | Developer/release owner with technical evidence; specialist advice as needed |
| AI policy | Determine applicability to actual conversational features; prevent prohibited/harmful outputs and verify accessible in-app reporting where required | Development/QA team and release owner |
| Android and billing | Final package/signature/version/API/native compatibility, Play product authority, purchase/restore/pending/refund behavior and accurate paid-access claims | Development/QA/release team |
| Engineering release controls | Source/configuration-bound results, account isolation, deployed parity, recovery/alerts, EN/ES critical flows and accessibility | Development/QA/release team |
| Optional outside assurance | Professional privacy/legal or clinical/safety opinion for extra assurance or a specific unresolved expert question | Chosen qualified adviser; hiring is not a blanket launch requirement |

Google's medical-device and human-subject-research provisions are conditional. A generic planning app is not assigned those obligations solely because it has an emotional check-in or a safety exit. Any future diagnosis/treatment/research purpose needs a fresh applicability assessment. This is a scoped release-policy interpretation, not a guarantee of compliance with every jurisdiction's laws.

## Health classification disposition

The preceding Console pass selected both Stress management, relaxation, mental acuity and Mental and behavioral health. Reassessment supports the narrower wellness category for the current optional wellness goals and stress-aware planning adaptations. The inspected distress routes pause productivity responses and offer trusted-person/support-resource exits; they do not establish counseling, addiction treatment or a clinical mental-health service. Therefore remove Mental and behavioral health from the saved, unsubmitted declaration and retain the wellness category. This is the developer's evidence-based category assessment, not Google approval. Reassess if the actual feature purpose changes; do not choose no health features merely to avoid applicable disclosures.

Health categories and collected data are separate. Optional emotions that leave the device may require Health info disclosure depending on what is transmitted; local-only inputs do not automatically become off-device collection. Complete the payload/provider mapping and final Data safety reconciliation rather than treating a form checkbox or a source comment as runtime proof.

## Replacement acceptance records

Schema 2 requires six records: privacyDisclosureValidation, aiSafetyValidation, providerRetention, backendParity, cloudIsolationRestoreDeletion and billingRecovery. The reviewer field identifies the accountable assessor (which may be the owner/team), not a mandatory professional qualification or paid provider. Accepted records must include source/configuration scope, real dated evidence, file hashes and finding disposition. Empty, pending, stale, altered, cross-source or cross-configuration records still fail. An owner name by itself is not evidence.

Privacy validation must cover actual optional payloads and cancellation, context selection/withdrawal, account boundaries, provider retention/deletion, report retention and matching policies/Console answers. AI safety validation must cover EN/ES ordinary and distress/crisis routing, refusal/exit/reporting, malicious prompts, unsupported claims, cancellation and failure recovery. Automated results can be used; actual enabled-profile flows must also be verified. No clinical efficacy conclusion is implied.
