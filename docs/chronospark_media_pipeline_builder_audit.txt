SECTION 14
MEDIA GOVERNANCE AUDIT
============================================================

Recommended rules:
- naming: consistent snake_case for all assets; remove typos/inconsistent stems
- folders: strict category boundaries (backgrounds, icons, animations, audio, legal, tutorials)
- versioning: track large media revisions with changelog metadata
- compression: optimize PNG/JPG and verify no oversized source assets remain in runtime scope
- allowed formats: svg, jpg/jpeg, png, json(lottie/tutorial), wav, ttf; avoid unused formats
- when to use Lottie: short state-change feedback and focused onboarding emphasis
- when to avoid media: dense productivity flows where static clarity is superior

Governance posture:
- Good baseline, needs tighter enforcement of declared-vs-present assets and constant centralization.
