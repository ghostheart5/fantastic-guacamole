3. Old hard-coded behavior
- The audit finding originally identified a static const AccountConnectionState.
- That old behavior always implied fixed account truth instead of reading identity/runtime state.
- In practice, that meant the UI could present EMAIL as connected and GOOGLE as disconnected regardless of actual state.

4. New runtime-derived behavior
- accountConnectionProvider now reads identityAccountProvider at runtime.
- EMAIL status is derived from whether a real identity exists and whether the identity contains a usable email.
- GOOGLE status is derived only from reliable provider metadata when available.
- If Google cannot be verified from the current identity model, the provider does not pretend it is connected.

5. How EMAIL status is derived
- Source: identityAccountProvider / ChronoSparkIdentity.
- Rule:
  - If identity exists and identity.email.trim().isNotEmpty, EMAIL is connected.
  - Otherwise EMAIL is disconnected.
- Labels are adjusted to stay truthful:
  - No identity available
  - Identity missing email
  - Primary Identity

6. How GOOGLE status is derived
- Source: ChronoSparkIdentity.authProvider plus Env.isSupabaseConfigured as a safe environment check.
- Rule:
  - If authProvider is ChronoSparkAuthProvider.google, GOOGLE is connected.
  - If no reliable Google signal exists, GOOGLE is not claimed as connected.
  - When Supabase is configured but no Google signal is present, the provider uses a pending/unknown-safe outcome rather than a false connected claim.
- Comment in the provider clarifies that Google is only marked connected with an explicit provider signal.

7. What happens when identity is missing
- EMAIL is disconnected.
- GOOGLE is disconnected or pending/unknown-safe depending on the environment path and the current provider state.
- No auth mutation is triggered.

8. What happens when email is missing
- EMAIL is disconnected.
- The label becomes Identity missing email.
- No logging, secrets, or user email output is produced.

9. What happens when Google provider metadata is unavailable
- GOOGLE is not marked connected.
- The provider returns a safe disconnected/pending outcome instead of inventing a connection.
- This preserves accuracy and avoids false trust signals.
