ChronoSpark Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 17, 2026
Product: ChronoSpark by GhostHeart5

ChronoSpark processes account email and authentication identifiers; planning
content you choose to save (tasks, goals, notes, reflections, recurring
planning items, Timeline entries, preferences, and progression); and limited
device, app, crash, and reliability diagnostics. A push token is stored only
when you enable notifications. We do not collect contacts or precise location
for planning features.

Planning data can remain local to your device. If you sign in and enable cloud
sync or backup, selected account data is stored in the GhostHeart5 Supabase
project under your account. Account data is not shared between users.

Smart Planner and SI Console use on-device guidance where available. An
external AI request is optional and sends only minimized context after you opt
in. Reporting an AI response sends only that response and your selected reason
for safety review; it does not send your prompt or conversation history.

Supabase may provide authentication, account data, and Edge Functions. Firebase
may provide aggregate analytics, crash diagnostics, and push delivery after
permission. Google Play services may provide distribution and purchase
entitlement processing. Each provider receives only the data needed for its
function. ChronoSpark does not sell personal information.

Delete your account from Settings > Account or use the public account-deletion
page. Account-scoped cloud data is removed through the approved deletion flow;
legally required or aggregated records may remain for the required period.
Local data can be cleared with the app's local-data controls.

Do not enter passwords, access tokens, payment credentials, medical records,
or other highly sensitive secrets into general planning fields.

Privacy and support requests: support@chronospark.app
