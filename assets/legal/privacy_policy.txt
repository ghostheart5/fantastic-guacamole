Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 12, 2026

This policy describes the data ChronoSpark may process in the contained public configuration and in eligible private internal-testing builds. Feature availability depends on the installed build and account eligibility. A listed provider is not proof that every provider feature is enabled.

Age and intended audience

ChronoSpark is intended for adults ages 18 and over. It is not designed for or directed to children. Do not create an account or use ChronoSpark if you are under 18.

Information you provide

- Account identifiers used for sign-in, such as an email address or sign-in provider identifier.

- Planning content you intentionally create, including tasks, planning context, goals, notes, schedules, preferences, and reflections.

- Support or account-deletion messages you choose to send.

Information needed for app functions

- Authentication and security events needed to protect an account.

- Notification device tokens only when messaging features are initialized for notifications.

- Purchase or entitlement data only when a supported Google Play billing flow is used.

- Local diagnostics needed to display and recover from an error on the device.

Current release containment

External AI

Disabled in the contained public configuration. Eligible private internal testers can enable disclosed AI-credit tests. Separately gated Planner explanations send only the selected planning content described before confirmation, when that feature is available. See the external AI details below.

Cloud sync

Disabled for the release candidate.

Cloud restore

Disabled for the release candidate.

Subscriptions and credits

Disabled in the contained public configuration. Eligible private internal-testing builds can offer Google Play subscriptions, credit top-ups, and confirmed credit spending. Purchases in this testing cohort require an eligible license tester and a Google Play test payment method; internal-track enrollment alone does not make a purchase free.

Analytics

Disabled by default and release-contained off.

Crash reporting

Disabled by default and release-contained off. Raw account identifiers must not be attached to crash reports if this changes in a future release.

Local and backend storage

ChronoSpark stores data on the device so core planning functions can work locally. Account and security services may use Supabase for authentication and approved account-scoped backend operations. When billing or AI-credit testing is enabled, Supabase also processes purchase-verification records, credit balances, usage, reservations, settlements, and retry metadata. These operations are separate from cloud sync and restore of planning data, which remain disabled.

Service providers

ChronoSpark may use Supabase for authentication and approved backend operations, Firebase Cloud Messaging for notifications, and Google Play for Android distribution and billing. Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics are release-contained off. Anthropic is the disclosed external AI provider for enabled private testing features. This is not an unrestricted public AI service.

External AI requests and your choice

Where an external AI action is enabled, ChronoSpark identifies the provider, the data to be sent, and the expected credit cost before asking you to confirm. The request passes through ChronoSpark's service to Anthropic only after confirmation. You can decline and continue using local planning guidance.

The internal credit-test controls submit a fixed fictional prompt, not your saved planning context, profile, or conversation history. A separately enabled Planner explanation submits selected visible plan clauses and relevant planning evidence disclosed for that request; it does not upload your whole planning library. The minimized explanation packet first goes to ChronoSpark's service for a quote. Confirming the quote permits sending that packet to Anthropic.

ChronoSpark retains usage, credit-reservation, settlement, and retry records for those operations. The optional Planner explanation service may keep response content for the short replay window shown with its quote, then retain billing metadata. Provider processing is also subject to Anthropic's applicable policies. Do not include passwords, payment credentials, or other secrets in submitted content. Local SI Console guidance and core planning do not require an external AI request.

ChronoSpark's Anthropic organization uses 30-day retention by default and does not use Zero Data Retention. Anthropic may retain data longer for usage-policy enforcement, legal requirements, or other exceptions described in its commercial data-retention policy. Requests may be processed globally. Optional sharing of prompts and responses as user feedback is disabled for this organization. ChronoSpark's shorter replay-content window does not shorten the provider's separate retention period.

Reporting an SI response

If you choose to report an SI response, we receive the selected response, your reporting reason and your account identifier. The selected response may include planning or other personal details that appear in it. We store the report to review safety, privacy and accuracy concerns; it is not processed only in memory. The reporting service does not forward the report to our external AI provider. Report records are linked to your account and are deleted from the report table when your account is deleted. The general retention and deletion terms in this policy also apply.

Optional voice input

Where voice input is available, ChronoSpark explains speech-provider processing before you agree to dictate. Your device's selected speech-recognition provider may send microphone audio to its servers for transcription under its own privacy policy. Availability, processing location, and provider retention depend on that service; ChronoSpark does not promise on-device-only transcription. You can decline and type instead. ChronoSpark does not store audio recordings or capture audio during normal planning or background use.

Recognized text appears in the input field for your review and is not automatically submitted. If you choose to send or save that text, the same content and storage rules apply as for typed text. Device speech processing is separate from ChronoSpark's external AI actions and does not spend ChronoSpark AI credits.

Retention and deletion

Account-linked information is retained only as needed to provide the active service, satisfy legal obligations, prevent fraud or abuse, resolve disputes, and protect security. Use the account-deletion page to review in-app and external request paths. Device-local data on an offline device may require clearing app storage or uninstalling the app.

Your choices

- Control available notifications and privacy settings in the app.

- Request access, correction, or deletion of account-linked information.

- Do not send passwords, one-time codes, recovery tokens, payment details, or secret keys in a support request.

Security and policy changes

ChronoSpark uses technical and organizational safeguards appropriate to the service, but no system is completely secure. Material policy changes will be posted at this URL with an updated date.
