Privacy PolicyChronoSpark Privacy PolicyEffective date: August 17, 2026. ChronoSpark is a GhostHeart5 product. This policy explains how information is handled for planning, account access, app reliability, optional intelligence, and support.

1. Information We CollectWhen you use ChronoSpark, we may process your account email and authentication identifier; tasks, goals, notes, reflections, recurring planning items, Timeline entries, preferences, and progression data that you choose to save; and device, app, crash, and diagnostic information needed for reliability. If you enable reminders, a push token may be stored for that account. We do not collect contacts or precise location for the planning features.

2. Local and Cloud ProcessingPlanning data is stored locally by default, including tasks, goals, Timeline records, notes, reflections, reminders, preferences, progression history, and local intelligence context. Cloud backup is a separate Settings control and is off until you enable it. When enabled and signed in, the current backup includes tasks, profile state, and settings; it does not promise a copy of every local cache or intelligence record. Backups are encrypted when the device secure store is available, scoped to your GhostHeart5 Supabase account, and never intentionally shared between users.

3. Optional Intelligence and ReportsSmart Planner and SI Console use available authorized planning evidence and show when evidence is limited. Deterministic guidance is the default. External AI is release-disabled. If it is later enabled after independent safety and provider-retention gates, one optional, read-only Smart Planner explanation may send visible deterministic plan clauses and visible evidence summaries through ChronoSpark's first-party Supabase function to Anthropic. This is data minimization, not sanitization, anonymization, or a promise that the text cannot be personal. Before sending to Anthropic, the app must show the provider, server-selected model label, exact data categories, and expected AI-credit cost. The explanation cannot change, save, schedule, or create anything, and it cannot replace Planner V2's deterministic decision. ChronoSpark may retain response content for up to five minutes for idempotent replay, then scrub that content to billing metadata. Anthropic account-level retention or zero-data-retention status has not been verified, so the feature must remain unavailable for release. Never enter passwords, access tokens, payment credentials, or highly sensitive secrets into general planning fields.

4. Services We UseThe GhostHeart5 Supabase project is the authorized production boundary for authentication, account data, account-scoped backup, and Edge Functions; Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics support minimized aggregate usage and reliability diagnostics; Firebase Cloud Messaging supports push reminders after permission; and Google Play services support distribution and purchase entitlement processing. Anthropic is the disclosed provider for the optional external Planner explanation only if its release gates are later satisfied; that path is currently disabled. Crash reports may include a limited recent event trail. Support diagnostics are user-triggered, shown as a reviewable summary, and omit stable device identifiers by default. Each service receives only the data needed for its stated function.

5. How We Use InformationProvide sign-in, account recovery, planning, Timeline, Creator, Progression, and strategic-intelligence features.Schedule reminders only after you grant notification permission.Protect accounts, prevent abuse, investigate safety reports, and restore your data.Measure reliability and improve performance using minimized diagnostics.

6. Retention and DeletionYou can delete your account from Settings > Account, or use the account deletion page. Account deletion removes the account and account-scoped cloud data through the approved deletion flow; recovery backups and abuse-prevention records may remain for up to 30 days where needed, and legally required or aggregated diagnostics may remain for the period required by law. Local data can be removed at any time from Settings > Clear Local Data; signing out also clears account-scoped local state. Turning off Cloud Backup stops future backup uploads but does not erase an existing cloud account—use account deletion or support for that.

7. ContactFor privacy requests, deletion questions, or support, email support@chronospark.app or use the support page.
