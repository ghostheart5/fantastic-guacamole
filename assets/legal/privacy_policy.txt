Axiomara: Decision OS

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

This policy explains how Axiomara handles data in the Android app and its account, billing, notification, and optional AI services.

Audience

Axiomara is intended for adults ages 18 and over. It is not designed for or directed to children.

Data you provide

- Account details such as email address, sign-in provider identifier, display name, and optional profile-image URL.

- Content you create, including tasks, goals, routines, notes, schedules, preferences, energy and emotional-state entries, planning context, reflections, and progress.

- Questions, selected records, recent conversation context, scenarios, and feedback you choose to submit to enabled AI or response-reporting features.

- Messages you send for support, privacy, or account deletion.

Data used for app functions

- Authentication and security events used to protect your account.

- Notification device tokens when you enable notifications.

- App-marketplace purchase, subscription, entitlement, and transaction identifiers when you use billing.

- Credit balances, quotes, reservations, settlements, retries, and usage records for paid AI requests.

- Local diagnostic information needed to show or recover from an error on your device.

Storage and service providers

Core planning content is stored on your device. Axiomara uses Supabase for authentication and approved account-scoped backend operations. Billing verification and AI-credit operations may store account-linked purchase and usage records. Firebase Cloud Messaging may process a device token for notifications. The app marketplace processes distribution and billing.

Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics are disabled in the current release configuration. Axiomara does not display ads, require users to watch ads, or sell personal information for advertising.

Optional encrypted cloud backup

Cloud backup and recovery are optional when available in your app version. Cloud Backup is off by default for each account. If you turn it on, Axiomara sends an account-scoped backup of tasks, profile information, and account preferences to Supabase. The backup payload is encrypted on your device before upload. Supabase also receives account-linked backup metadata needed to store and synchronize it.

This cloud backup does not include other planning data such as goals, habits, notes, occurrence history, decision outcomes, memories, timeline history, or Person Context. These records remain on your device, cannot be restored from this cloud backup, and may be lost after uninstalling the app or losing the device. A separately exported local backup has its own data coverage; it is not an automatic cloud copy.

The backup encryption key is kept in the app's local secure storage. You can export a recovery key and keep it separately, then import it on another device before restoring. Axiomara does not upload that recovery key with your cloud backup. If you lose both the local key and your recovery copy, the encrypted backup cannot be restored.

You can turn Cloud Backup off in Settings to stop future automatic transfers. Turning it off does not delete a backup already stored in the cloud. Account deletion removes the account's active cloud backup and associated Storage objects; service backups and records required for security, billing disputes, or legal obligations follow the retention rules below.

Optional external AI

When an external AI action is available, Axiomara shows the provider, the categories of information to be sent, and the expected credit cost before confirmation. If you confirm, the request passes through Axiomara's service to Anthropic. You can decline and continue using non-generative planning features.

Enabled AI conversation tools may submit your question, up to six recent conversation messages, and the limited tasks, goals, milestones, timeline records, note, energy input, emotional state, filter, date range, mode, or scenario selected for that request. A separately confirmed explanation may submit the visible plan clauses and evidence selected for explanation. These requests do not upload your complete planning library or silently change saved records.

Axiomara retains billing and usage records needed to quote, reserve, settle, retry, and reconcile credits. The visible conversation remains available while its screen remains open; Axiomara does not provide a saved transcript archive. Anthropic's commercial service uses a default 30-day retention period and may retain information longer for policy enforcement, security, or legal obligations. See Anthropic's retention information.

Response reports

If you report an AI response, Axiomara receives the selected response, your reason, and your account identifier. The response may contain planning or personal details. Reports are stored for safety, privacy, and accuracy review and are deleted from the report table when your account is deleted.

Voice input

If you choose voice input, your device's speech-recognition provider may process microphone audio under its own terms. Axiomara does not store audio recordings. Recognized text is placed in the input field for review and is not automatically submitted. Voice transcription does not use Axiomara AI credits.

Retention, security, and deletion

Account-linked information is retained only as needed to provide the service, maintain security, prevent fraud or abuse, resolve disputes, and meet legal obligations. No system is completely secure. Use the account-deletion page to request deletion in the app or without the app. Data stored only on an offline device may require clearing app storage or uninstalling the app.

Your choices

- Review and decline an external AI request before information is sent.

- Control available notification and privacy settings in the app.

- Request access, correction, or deletion of account-linked information.

- Do not send passwords, one-time codes, recovery tokens, payment details, or secret keys in support messages.
