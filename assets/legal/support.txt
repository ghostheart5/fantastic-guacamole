Support

Contact path: Email support is available for access, billing, privacy, deletion, and technical issues.

Email Axiomara support at ghostheart131517@gmail.com

What to include

- Axiomara version, device model, and Android version.

- The screen, action, and visible error.

- Short reproduction steps and a screenshot when appropriate.

- Your sign-in method, without a password, one-time code, recovery token, payment credential, or secret key.

Install and update Axiomara

Axiomara is available on Google Play. Install and manage updates through the store using the account on your device. If the listing says the app is unavailable, include your country, device model, Android version, and the message shown when contacting support. This site does not distribute APK files.

Billing and AI credits

The app marketplace controls subscription and credit-pack purchases. Manage or cancel a subscription in the marketplace account that made the purchase. Deleting the app or your Axiomara account does not cancel store billing.

An enabled external AI action shows its provider, submitted data categories, and credit quote before confirmation. For a missing entitlement, balance, or settlement, include the app version, product or request, approximate time, and visible error. Never email payment credentials.

Account deletion

Use the in-app deletion control when you can sign in. The account-deletion page provides an external request path when you cannot use the app.
