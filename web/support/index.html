ChronoSpark Support

Last updated: August 9, 2026

For account, purchase, privacy, tester, or technical support, use the developer contact shown on the ChronoSpark Google Play listing.

Include in a technical request

  • Your ChronoSpark version, device model, and Android version.
  • The feature and screen where the problem occurred.
  • Short reproduction steps and a screenshot when appropriate.
  • The sign-in provider used, but never a password or verification code.

Account deletion

Use the in-app account controls or follow the instructions on the account-deletion page. A remote deletion request must not contain passwords, recovery codes, or payment credentials.

Subscriptions

Purchases and subscription cancellation are managed through Google Play. You can review or cancel an active subscription in Google Play subscriptions.

Privacy

The Privacy Policy describes the information used by ChronoSpark and the available user choices.
