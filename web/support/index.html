Support

Last updated: August 31, 2026

Working contact path: Email is available for account, privacy, tester, and technical support. Do not include a password, one-time code, recovery token, payment credential, or secret key.

Email ChronoSpark support

Include in a technical request

  • ChronoSpark version, device model, and Android version.
  • The screen and action where the problem occurred.
  • Short reproduction steps and a screenshot when appropriate.
  • The sign-in method used, without sending credentials or verification codes.

Invited testing

Use only the Google Play test link associated with the Google account that was invited. This public site does not claim an open Play listing or provide an unofficial APK.

Account deletion

Use the in-app deletion control when you can sign in. The account-deletion page also provides an external email request path.

Purchases

Subscriptions and AI-credit purchases are not enabled for the current release candidate. If a future Play purchase exists, manage store billing through the Google Play account that made the purchase.

Response expectations

Include a safe reply address. Account or deletion requests may require limited identity verification before account-linked information can be discussed or changed.
