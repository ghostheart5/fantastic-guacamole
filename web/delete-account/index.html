Delete a ChronoSpark Account

Last updated: August 9, 2026

Account deletion is permanent. It removes the ChronoSpark account and account-linked data after the request has been verified.

Delete from the app

  1. Open ChronoSpark and sign in.
  2. Open Settings, then the account and security controls.
  3. Choose Delete account.
  4. Complete the requested identity verification and confirm deletion.

Request deletion without the app

Use the developer contact shown on the ChronoSpark Google Play listing. Send the request from, or identify, the email address or phone number tied to the account and use the subject ChronoSpark account deletion. We may request limited additional verification before deleting an account. Never send a password, one-time code, recovery token, or payment credential.

Data deleted

  • The authentication account and profile.
  • Account-linked planning content, settings, progress, and cloud files.
  • Account-linked notification-device registrations.

Device-local data

A request made from the web cannot directly erase files on a device that is offline or no longer connected to the account. After remote deletion, clear ChronoSpark's app storage or uninstall the app on each device you no longer control. The in-app deletion flow also attempts to remove local account data from the device where it is performed.

Limited retention

Limited transaction, tax, fraud-prevention, security, or dispute records may be retained when required by law or necessary to protect the service. Such records are restricted to those purposes and are not used to recreate a deleted ChronoSpark account.

Subscriptions are separate

Deleting a ChronoSpark account does not cancel an active Google Play subscription. Review or cancel it separately in Google Play subscriptions.

Processing time

Verified requests are processed without unreasonable delay and ordinarily within 30 days. We will communicate if applicable law or a security review requires additional time.
