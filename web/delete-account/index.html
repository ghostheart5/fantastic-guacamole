Axiomara: Decision OS

Delete an Axiomara Account

Last updated: September 18, 2026

Permanent action: Verified deletion removes the authentication account and eligible account-linked data. It cannot be undone.

Delete in the app

  1. Open Axiomara and sign in.
  2. Open Settings and find the account and security controls.
  3. Select Delete account.
  4. Complete identity verification and confirm deletion.

Request deletion without the app

Email from the address connected to the account when possible. State that you want your Axiomara account and associated data deleted. Limited identity verification may be required.

Request account deletion at ghostheart131517@gmail.com

Never send a password, one-time code, recovery token, payment credential, or secret key.

Data covered

  • The authentication account and account profile.
  • Eligible account-linked planning content, settings, progress, AI response reports, and backend records.
  • Account-linked notification-device registrations.

Device-local data

A web request cannot erase files on an offline device. After remote deletion, clear Axiomara app storage or uninstall the app on devices you no longer use.

Limited retention

Transaction, tax, fraud-prevention, security, dispute, and credit-usage records may be retained when required by law or necessary to protect the service. Retained records are restricted to those purposes and are not used to recreate a deleted account.

Marketplace billing

Deleting an Axiomara account does not cancel an app-marketplace purchase or subscription. Cancel an active subscription separately through the marketplace account that made the purchase.

Timing

Verified requests are processed without unreasonable delay and ordinarily within 30 days. Additional time may be required by applicable law or a security review.
