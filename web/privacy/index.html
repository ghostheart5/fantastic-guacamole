ChronoSpark Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 9, 2026

This policy describes how ChronoSpark handles information when you use the Android app and its supporting services.

Information you provide

  • Account identifiers, such as your email address, phone number, or sign-in provider identifier.
  • Planning content you create in Nexus, Smart Planner, Creator, SI Console, Timeline, Trajectory Engine, and Progression.
  • Visible deterministic plan clauses and evidence summaries you explicitly confirm before using the optional external Planner explanation. This minimized text is not guaranteed to be sanitized, anonymized, or non-personal.
  • Support and account-deletion communications you send to us.

Information processed automatically

  • Authentication and security events needed to protect your account.
  • Purchase and entitlement status supplied by Google Play.
  • Device messaging tokens used for notifications you enable.
  • App diagnostics, crash reports, and limited usage events when the relevant telemetry setting is enabled.

Local and cloud storage

ChronoSpark stores some data on your device so that core features can work offline. If cloud features are enabled, account-linked data may also be stored and processed by ChronoSpark's backend services.

How information is used

  • Authenticate users and protect accounts.
  • Provide, synchronize, restore, and improve app functionality.
  • Generate a separately confirmed, read-only Planner explanation without changing or saving the deterministic plan.
  • Verify purchases and manage feature entitlements.
  • Send requested notifications and investigate reliability or security problems.

Service providers

ChronoSpark uses Supabase for authentication, database, storage, and Edge Functions; Firebase for messaging, analytics, and crash reporting; Google Play for Android distribution and billing; and Anthropic as the disclosed provider for the optional external Planner explanation if its release gates are satisfied. External AI is currently release-disabled. Before a future request, ChronoSpark must show the provider, server-selected model label, transmitted data categories, and expected AI-credit cost. Provider account-level retention or zero-data-retention status has not been verified, so this path must remain unavailable for release. ChronoSpark does not sell your personal information.

Retention and deletion

Account-linked content is retained while needed to provide the service. If the optional external Planner explanation is later enabled, ChronoSpark may retain its response content for no more than a five-minute idempotency window and then scrub it to billing metadata. This first-party rule does not claim or prove Anthropic's account-level retention. When a verified deletion request is completed, account-linked data is deleted or de-identified except for limited records that must be retained for legal, tax, fraud-prevention, security, or dispute-resolution reasons. Device-local copies are controlled separately as explained on the account-deletion page.

Your choices

  • Use the controls in the app to manage notifications and available telemetry settings.
  • Request access, correction, or deletion of account-linked information.
  • Cancel a subscription through Google Play; deleting an account does not cancel store billing.

Security and policy changes

ChronoSpark uses technical and organizational safeguards appropriate to the service. No system is completely secure. Material policy changes will be posted at this URL with an updated date.

Contact

Use the developer contact shown on the ChronoSpark Google Play listing or visit the support page. Never send a password, recovery code, or secret key in a support request.
