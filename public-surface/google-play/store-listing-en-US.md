## App name

Axiomara: Daily Planner

## Short description

Plan your next step around your goals, available time, and energy

## Full description

When your list is long and your time is short, Axiomara helps you choose one realistic next step around your goals, available time, and energy.

START WITH ONE USEFUL ACTION
Capture a task, review the proposed change, and confirm what you want to save. Fit it into your schedule and mark it complete when you finish. You stay in control of what to accept, change, delay, or reject.

PLAN AROUND REAL LIFE
Keep tasks, goals, notes, and habits in one place. Consider your available time, priorities, schedule pressure, and the information you choose to share. Ask for a smaller step or explore another approach when your day changes.

UNDERSTAND THE RECOMMENDATION
Today gives you a place to start. Choose a step offers planning guidance, and you review proposed changes before saving them. Insights shows patterns, predictions, and possible future outcomes so you can compare options. Predictions are possibilities, not promises.

REVIEW WHAT HAPPENED
Activity shows recorded completions and progress. Profile shows your recorded activity, level, and XP. Optional check-ins let you describe your current energy and how you feel.

PLAN WITHOUT ADS
Axiomara has no banners or rewarded videos. Ad-free planning does not require a subscription.

OPTIONAL AI AND PAID FEATURES
External AI actions are optional. Before you confirm one, Axiomara identifies the provider, the selected context that may be sent, and the expected credit cost. You can decline and continue with non-generative planning. Subscription access and spendable AI credits are shown separately. Available paid features and prices appear before purchase.

OPTIONAL CLOUD BACKUP
Cloud Backup is off by default. Automatic and older backups may include only tasks, profile information, and account preferences. When you choose “Back up all planning data” and confirm, the encrypted snapshot also includes goals, habits, notes, occurrence history, decision outcomes, and schedule history. This replaces the previous cloud snapshot with this device’s data. Restore the latest snapshot before backing up from a replacement device, and keep your recovery key separately. Private memories, personal information shared for guidance, and local Chat history are excluded from these cloud snapshots.

Axiomara is for adults 18 and over. It supports planning and decisions; it does not replace professional, medical, legal, financial, or emergency support.
