# Choose a step

> Guide updated October 23, 2026. Screen names can vary by installed version. The October 22–23 repairs are tested and merged; this does not establish that a new Google Play build is available. See [[Release Notes|Release-Notes]].

Choose a step helps you pick one next step, in both modes: "On this device · free" and AI chat. One switch at the top moves between them, and your notes go with you. Ask about a specific situation and choose the records or context you want to use. A useful request is: “Help me find time for my grocery task today. My appointment ends at 5:45 and the store closes at 8.”

## Before sending

Review the selected context, consent, availability and displayed credit quote. With "On this device · free", your notes stay on this device and nothing is charged. With AI chat, your question and the information shown before sending leave your device: Axiomara checks the price, then an external AI service writes the reply. You can continue managing your plan without buying a question.

## Read the response critically

Check which evidence supports the answer, which details are assumptions, what is missing and whether the proposed next step fits your day. A response can be wrong. Correct false assumptions rather than treating confident wording as proof.

Suggestions do not silently move, complete or remove your records. Review any separate save or apply action. For deeper investigation see [[Chat|SI-CONSOLE]].

## History and privacy

Keep follow-ups in the relevant conversation and review context before sending again. The October 23 native check confirmed one historical nonempty question/reply pair remained visible after the update; it did not prove every message, a new paid response, or all process-death cases on that build.

AI output is decision support, not medical, legal, financial or emergency advice. [[Settings]] explains credits and privacy controls.
