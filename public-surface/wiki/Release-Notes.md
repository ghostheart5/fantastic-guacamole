# Release Notes

Axiomara is in controlled testing. Release notes focus on behavior that testers can observe and verify.

## Current testing focus

- Axiomara identity and the Human Decision OS model across product documentation.
- Context-grounded Smart Planner conversations with follow-up continuity.
- SI Console entity filters, scenarios, query controls, and Advanced analysis.
- Alignment of Energy, Clarity, and Momentum across Nexus and Trajectory.
- Explainable Progression evidence, levels, experience, and leverage actions.
- Accessibility, account control, privacy, billing safety, and no-ad behavior.

## How to report a useful issue

Include:

1. the screen and control you used;
2. the question or data you entered, with private details removed;
3. the response or behavior you observed;
4. what you expected instead; and
5. whether retrying or changing context affected the result.

Do not include passwords, authentication codes, payment details, or information you are not authorized to share.
