> Guide updated October 23, 2026. Screen names can vary by installed version. The October 22–23 repairs are tested and merged; this does not establish that a new Google Play build is available. See [[Release Notes|Release-Notes]].

Use SI Chat for a decision that benefits from examining evidence, assumptions, constraints and trade-offs. Start with what you are deciding, what options you see and what a useful answer should help you do.

Select only relevant context. Review the displayed credit quote and consent before a cloud question. Free tools are separate from paid questions. Eligible monthly credits are account-specific; Settings is the reference for your own balance and reset.

Read the result for evidence, uncertainty and a realistic next step. Do not treat a generated explanation as a diagnosis or a guarantee. A scenario is a preview; it does not edit your saved plan or authorize purchases.
