# Daily Workflow

Axiomara is designed to support a flexible operating loop: notice what matters, choose a useful next action, capture new information when it appears, and adjust as reality changes.

It is not a rigid morning routine or a requirement to visit every feature every day.

## First-time setup

For a new user, Axiomara guides the core setup path as:

```text
Creator → Timeline → Nexus
```

1. In [[Creator]], create one useful item: a task, goal, Daily Rhythm, or note.
2. In [[Timeline]], review the item as scheduled action.
3. In [[Nexus]], see the current operating state and a next-best action.

This setup path gives the system enough context to begin supporting your decisions. It does not require you to build a perfect plan before you act.

## The ongoing daily loop

Once set up, start wherever helps you move forward. A practical loop is:

```text
Notice → choose → act → capture → review → adjust
```

The loop is intentionally flexible. Some days you may only check Nexus and complete one task. Other days you may need to capture a new idea, change a schedule, or review a plan.

## Notice what matters

Use [[Nexus]] to understand your current operating state and identify a next-best action. Start with the smallest meaningful step you can actually take.

Nexus does not replace your judgment. Treat it as context for a decision, then choose the action that is appropriate for your situation.

## Plan and schedule when useful

Use [[Timeline]] to plan, review, and execute scheduled action. Creator owns tasks, goals, Daily Rhythms, and notes; Timeline is their scheduling projection, not a second authoring surface.

Use [[Smart Planner]] when you want explainable planning assistance or help reconciling your inputs into a feasible plan. It is optional—not a required daily step—and its output should be reviewed before you rely on it.

## Act on the next useful step

Work on the action that is realistic and valuable now. A useful next step may be a scheduled task, a small action that advances a goal, or a Daily Rhythm you want to maintain.

Completing a small action is often more valuable than extending the planning process.

## Capture when new information appears

Use [[Creator]] whenever a task, goal, Daily Rhythm, or note needs a home in the system. Capture can happen before, during, or after planned work; it is not limited to a separate “execution” phase.

Start with the smallest useful version of the item and refine it later if needed.

## Review and adjust

Use Timeline to review scheduled action and use a [[Notes]] or Reflection when you want to preserve context, observations, or a lesson from the day.

Adjustment is part of the workflow. Reschedule, refine, or retire work that no longer fits. A missed item is useful information—not proof that the whole plan failed.

## Use advanced surfaces deliberately

- [[SI Console]] supports deeper strategic investigation and executable guidance when you need it.
- Trajectory Engine examines explicit future scenarios, assumptions, and corrections.
- Progression focuses on evidence-backed advancement and leverage actions.

These surfaces can add useful perspective, but they are not mandatory daily steps and do not guarantee an automatic recommendation or outcome. Their value depends on the information available and your own review.

## A healthy daily workflow

A healthy workflow is one you can return to:

- Keep the next action small and clear.
- Capture new information before it gets lost.
- Schedule only what benefits from a place in time.
- Review what happened without treating a missed day as failure.
- Adjust the system to fit your life, rather than forcing your life to fit the system.
