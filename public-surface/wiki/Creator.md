# Creator

Creator is Axiomara's intelligent intake workspace. It is where an intention becomes a usable part of your system: a task to do, a goal to pursue, a Daily Rhythm to repeat, or a note to keep.

Its job is simple: make the first step fast and clear, so that capture does not become a barrier to action.

## What you can create

Creator supports four core entry types:

- **Tasks** — specific actions that need to be completed.
- **Goals** — outcomes you want to work toward.
- **Daily Rhythms** — repeatable behaviors and routines that support consistency.
- **Notes** — ideas, context, reflection, and information worth keeping.

Choose the type that best fits what you know now. You can add detail as you go; you do not need a perfect plan before capturing the first item.

## Capture first, refine later

Creator follows a low-friction principle:

> Capture first. Refine later.

Start with the smallest useful version of the item. A task can begin as “Book dentist appointment.” A goal can begin as “Improve fitness.” A note can preserve an idea before it disappears. Creator gives that thought a place in the system without requiring you to organize everything at once.

Where available, Creator can also receive a voice handoff and carry suggested scheduling or frequency context into the entry. Review the saved item before relying on any suggestion.

## From capture to action

Creator owns the inputs. [[Timeline]] is the scheduling and execution projection for those inputs, not a second place to author the same item.

For a new user, the core activation path is:

```text
Creator → Timeline → Nexus
```

After you create your first item, Axiomara can direct you to Timeline to review it in context. Once your Timeline is set up, [[Nexus]] becomes the place to see the current operating state and the next best action.

For ongoing use, the path is flexible: capture something in Creator, schedule or review it in Timeline when needed, then use Nexus to stay oriented and execute.

## How Creator connects to the system

Creator provides the structured inputs that make the rest of Axiomara more useful.

- [[Nexus]] turns your current inputs and state into situational awareness and a next-best action.
- [[Timeline]] helps you review, schedule, and execute Creator-owned items.
- [[Smart Planner]] can reconcile planning context into an explainable plan.
- [[SI Console]] uses relevant context for deeper strategic investigation and guidance; it is not required before you can create or act on an item.
- [[Trajectory Engine]] and [[Progression]] use the broader system context to examine future direction and evidence-backed progress.

Good inputs improve planning, but Creator does not promise that every entry will automatically become a recommendation, schedule, or insight. Review and choose the next action that fits your situation.

## Tasks

Use a task for a concrete action.

Examples:

- Schedule a dentist appointment
- Finish the budget review
- Call a client
- Complete a workout

Tasks can support a goal, but a useful task should still describe something you can actually do.

## Goals

Use a goal for a desired outcome that may require multiple actions or rhythms.

Examples:

- Launch a business
- Improve physical health
- Complete a certification
- Publish a book

Goals provide direction. Supporting tasks and Daily Rhythms turn that direction into practical work.

The word “mission” may appear as strategic context elsewhere in Axiomara. It is not a separate Creator entry type or a dedicated Creator screen.

## Daily Rhythms

Use a Daily Rhythm for a behavior you want to repeat consistently.

Examples:

- Morning planning
- Daily exercise
- Evening reflection
- Read before bed

Daily Rhythms can support goals, but they are not merely a checklist. They represent the repeatable cadence that helps sustained work happen.

**Terminology note:** Axiomara’s public name is **Daily Rhythms**. Some internal or older implementation labels may use “habit,” but public documentation should use Daily Rhythms.

## Notes

Use a note when you need to preserve information without forcing it into a task, goal, or rhythm.

Examples:

- Project research
- Meeting notes
- Business ideas
- Weekly reflection

Notes can add useful context to later planning, but saving a note does not automatically create work or a recommendation.

## What Creator is not

Creator is not:

- A dashboard or reporting screen
- An analytics surface
- A guidance engine
- A duplicate scheduling surface

Those responsibilities belong to connected parts of the product such as Nexus, Timeline, Smart Planner, and SI Console. Creator is the clear starting point for getting structured information into Axiomara.
