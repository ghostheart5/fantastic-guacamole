# Today

> Guide updated October 23, 2026. Screen names can vary by installed version. The October 22–23 repairs are tested and merged; this does not establish that a new Google Play build is available. See [[Release Notes|Release-Notes]].

Today is the starting point for reviewing your day and choosing a manageable next step. Use your real commitments and available capacity as context rather than trying to satisfy a score.

Add something when you need a new task, goal, habit or note. Open My plan to review records and Schedule to work with dates. Record a completion only after the action happens, then review Activity and Insights for feedback.

Recommendations remain suggestions. You can revise or ignore them, and missing or outdated records can affect their usefulness.
