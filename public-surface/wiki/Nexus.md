# Nexus

Nexus is Axiomara's home surface for understanding your **current operating state** and choosing a **next-best action**. It brings relevant planning context together so you can make a decision without treating every signal as equally urgent.

Nexus supports your judgment. It does not make decisions for you or replace what you know about your life and responsibilities.

## When to use Nexus

For a new user, the setup path is:

```text
Creator → Timeline → Nexus
```

After setup, use Nexus whenever you want to reconnect with what is current, what needs attention, and what a useful next move could be. It is not limited to a morning briefing or a fixed daily routine.

## What Nexus helps you understand

Nexus can bring together current signals from across Axiomara, including:

- A current operating decision and next-best action.
- Relevant warnings, risks, or conditions that may need attention.
- Recent progress and connected planning context.
- The availability and health of connected feature signals.
- Whether the system is ready, still loading, partially available, offline, or needs recovery.

The exact information depends on the inputs and evidence available in your system.

## Use the operating state before acting

Start with the operating state and any stated limitations. A useful sequence is:

1. Read the current next-best action and the context behind it.
2. Check whether Nexus reports a partial, offline, or unavailable state.
3. Choose the action that is realistic and meaningful now.
4. Open another surface only when it helps: capture, schedule, plan, investigate, compare scenarios, or review advancement.

If a signal is missing, stale, or unavailable, do not assume Nexus has complete context. Verify the underlying information or work from the evidence you do have.

## How Nexus connects the product

```text
Creator inputs + Timeline context + planning and strategic signals
                         ↓
       Nexus: current operating state + next-best action
                         ↓
                Your reviewed decision and action
```

Nexus is a decision hub, not a duplicate authoring or scheduling surface.

| Surface | How it connects to Nexus |
| --- | --- |
| [[Creator]] | Supplies tasks, goals, Daily Rhythms, and notes as structured inputs. Nexus reads relevant context; Creator remains the place to create and manage those inputs. |
| [[Timeline]] | Provides scheduled and operational context. Timeline remains the place to plan, review, and execute scheduled action. |
| [[Smart Planner]] | Provides explainable planning assistance and plan reconciliation when you want a feasible plan. |
| [[SI Console]] | Supports deeper strategic investigation and executable guidance when the question needs more context. |
| [[Trajectory Engine]] | Provides explicit future scenarios, assumptions, and corrections. |
| [[Progression]] | Provides evidence-backed advancement and leverage context. |

## Nexus and Momentum

Nexus can surface Momentum and Pressure as operating signals. They can help you notice current conditions, but they are not personal judgments or guaranteed forecasts.

If pressure is high or momentum is low, the best next action may be smaller, lighter, or focused on recovery. Use the signal to adjust the plan rather than forcing intensity.

For more detail, see [[Momentum]].

## What Nexus is not

Nexus is not:

- A replacement for Creator, Timeline, or the other product surfaces
- A guarantee that every connected signal is current or available
- A mandatory daily checklist
- A source of instructions that override your judgment
- A standalone prediction or progress score

Its purpose is clarity: help you see the most useful current context, then decide what to do next.
