# Frequently asked questions

> Guide updated October 23, 2026. Screen names can vary by installed version. The October 22–23 repairs are tested and merged; this does not establish that a new Google Play build is available. See [[Release Notes|Release-Notes]].

## What is Axiomara?
A reflection and planning app for tasks, goals, habits, notes, progress, Chat and modeled future outcomes. Start with one useful action rather than configuring every tool.

## Why do older guides use different names?
Navigation and wording have been simplified. The guides now use Today, Add something, My plan, Schedule, Chat, Insights and Activity. Older wiki addresses remain valid for bookmarks. Your installed public build may differ from the latest tested source.

## Does Compare change my task?
No. Complete, Delay and Remove are simulations in the comparison controls. Press Compare, then View comparison. Edit your real record separately if you choose to act. After changing draft inputs, compare again.

## Is everything AI or paid?
No. Free tools and planning are separate from cloud questions. Review the displayed quote before confirming a paid request. Eligible accounts may have included monthly credits; check Settings for your actual allowance, balance and reset. No unlimited-use or universal monthly-grant promise is made.

## Does a prediction guarantee what will happen?
No. It models recorded evidence and assumptions. Missing dates, little history and real-life changes can limit it.

## Will replaying the guide erase my plan?
The current guidance explains that guide replay keeps saved work and activity. Welcome/account setup is a separate entry and also explains retained work and guide progress. Neither should be confused with account deletion or recovery restore.

## Is a merged fix already in the store?
Not necessarily. [[Release Notes|Release-Notes]] separates tested source from public delivery. Use the store to install or update; this website does not distribute APKs.
