# habits

> Guide updated October 23, 2026. Screen names can vary by installed version. The October 22–23 repairs are tested and merged; this does not establish that a new Google Play build is available. See [[Release Notes|Release-Notes]].

habits are Axiomara's public name for repeatable actions and routines. They help you make a useful commitment visible, schedule it when appropriate, and revisit it as your real life changes.

They answer a practical question:

> What is worth doing again on a regular basis?

## Create a habit in Add something

habits are a Add something-owned input type. Start in [[Add something|Creator]], choose **habit**, give it a clear title, choose when it repeats, and save it.

Add something offers these repeat choices:

- **One-time** — useful when an item does not need to repeat.
- **Every day** — the default for a new habit.
- **Every week** — useful for recurring reviews, planning, or other weekly commitments.

You can also add a schedule date when the item belongs on your [[Schedule|Timeline]]. Schedule is the scheduling and execution projection for Add something-owned items; it is not a second place to create the same rhythm.

## habits, tasks, and goals

These three things work together, but they serve different purposes.

| Type | Use it for | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Task** | A concrete action to complete | Schedule a dentist appointment |
| **habit** | A repeatable action or routine | Take a 20-minute walk every day |
| **Goal** | An outcome that may need many actions | Improve physical health |

A habit can support a goal, but it does not have to. The important test is simple: can you describe a realistic action you are willing to repeat?

## Start small and make it specific

Effective habits are clear, realistic, and repeatable.

| Less useful | More useful |
| --- | --- |
| Get healthier | Walk for 10 minutes every day |
| Be more organized | Review tomorrow's priorities each evening |
| Read more | Read for 15 minutes before bed |

Small commitments are easier to review honestly. If a rhythm is too large, change it rather than treating a missed day as failure. The goal is a sustainable pattern, not a perfect streak.

## From rhythm to action

habits move through Axiomara as part of the wider planning system:

```text
Add something → Schedule → Today
```

- [[Add something|Creator]] holds the habit as a structured input.
- [[Schedule|Timeline]] helps you schedule and review the action in context.
- [[Today|Nexus]] uses your current inputs and state to surface situational awareness and a next-best action.

[[Chat|Smart Planner]] can use relevant planning context to help reconcile an explainable plan. [[SI Chat|SI Console]] can use relevant context for deeper strategic investigation and guidance. Neither surface guarantees a recommendation, schedule, or progress outcome for every habit; review your plan and choose the action that fits your situation.

[[Predictions|Trajectory Engine]] and [[Activity|Progression]] may use broader system context for future direction and evidence-backed progress.

## Review and adjust

Review a rhythm when it no longer fits your life or supports what matters:

- Is the action specific enough to do?
- Is the recurrence realistic?
- Does the schedule help, or create pressure without value?
- Does this rhythm still support a goal or priority you care about?

Adjusting or retiring an unhelpful rhythm is useful information, not a failure.

## Terminology

Use **habits** in Axiomara documentation and support material. Some internal or older implementation labels may say “habit” or “routine”; those are legacy technical terms and are not public product vocabulary.

“Mission” may appear as strategic context elsewhere in Axiomara, but it is not a habit record type, required workflow, or dedicated habit screen.
