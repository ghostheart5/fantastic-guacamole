# Getting Started

Axiomara becomes useful when it contains one honest piece of your life. You do not need to build a perfect system before it can help.

## Build the first operating loop

### 1. Capture one real record

Open [[Creator]] and create the smallest useful record:

- a **Task** for something you can complete;
- a **Goal** for an outcome that needs direction;
- a **Daily Rhythm** for something worth repeating; or
- a **Note** for context that should be remembered.

Example: create the task **Buy groceries**, include the items you need, and add any time constraint you already know.

### 2. Put action in time

Open [[Timeline]]. Add a date or time only when it helps you act. The record remains owned by Creator; Timeline shows its place in the day and its relationship to other commitments.

### 3. Orient in Nexus

Open [[Nexus]] and review Energy, Clarity, Momentum, current pressure, and the recommended next useful action. Treat the signals as context. You still choose what fits your actual circumstances.

### 4. Ask a grounded question

Open [[Smart Planner]], ask a specific question, and attach the relevant record:

> What time should I go to the store? Use my grocery task and today's schedule.

Read the evidence and assumptions, not only the recommended time. Ask a follow-up if a real-world condition changes.

### 5. Investigate when the decision is deeper

Use [[SI Console]] when you need to compare trade-offs, examine a risk, test a scenario, or understand why progress is blocked.

## A healthy daily loop

```text
Nexus → choose → act → record what changed → reflect → adjust
```

You might use every surface during a complex week or only Creator, Timeline, and Nexus on a simple day.

## Make the system tell the truth

- Keep dates and statuses current.
- Write tasks as actions, not vague aspirations.
- Keep goals broad enough to matter and specific enough to recognize progress.
- Use notes for context rather than manufacturing extra tasks.
- Reduce or reschedule a Daily Rhythm when it no longer fits.
- Correct Planner or SI when an assumption is wrong.
