For account, purchase, privacy, tester, or technical support, contact **ghostheart131517@gmail.com**.

## Include in a technical request

- Your Axiomara version, device model, and Android version.
- The feature and screen where the issue occurred.
- Short reproduction steps and a screenshot when appropriate.
- The sign-in provider used, if relevant.

Do not send passwords, verification or recovery codes, API keys, or payment credentials.
