Account Deletion
Last Updated: July 8, 2026

This page explains how to request deletion of your Axiomara account and associated account data.

Overview
Axiomara allows users to request permanent deletion of their account and associated cloud-linked data.

If you wish to permanently delete your account, please follow the instructions below.

How To Request Account Deletion
To request account deletion:

Send an email to ghostheart131517@gmail.com
Use a subject line such as:
Account Deletion Request
Include the email address associated with your Axiomara account.
This information is required to verify account ownership before any deletion request can be processed.

Verification
For security purposes, Axiomara may require verification that you are the owner of the account before deletion is performed.

Verification helps prevent unauthorized deletion requests.

What Will Be Deleted
When a verified deletion request is approved, Axiomara will delete:

Account profile information
Authentication-related account records
Cloud-linked user data associated with the account
Connected account settings where applicable
The goal is to remove account-related information that is no longer required to operate the service.

Local Device Data
Some data may exist only on your device.

Examples may include:

Cached information
Local preferences
Local application storage
Offline planning data
Removing the application from your device or clearing application storage may be required to remove locally stored information.

Deleting your account does not automatically delete all locally stored device data.

Information That May Be Retained
In certain circumstances, limited information may be retained when required for:

Legal compliance
Security investigations
Fraud prevention
Tax and accounting obligations
Dispute resolution
Regulatory requirements
Enforcement of platform policies
Any retained information will be limited to what is reasonably necessary for those purposes.

Processing Time
Verified account deletion requests are generally completed within:

30 Days
Actual processing times may vary depending on request volume and verification requirements.

Important Information
Please review the following before submitting a deletion request:

Account Deletion Is Permanent
Deleted accounts may not be recoverable.

Once account data has been removed, restoration may not be possible.

App Removal Is Not Account Deletion
Deleting or uninstalling the Axiomara application does not automatically remove your account.

A deletion request must be submitted separately.

Active Subscriptions
If applicable, users should manage or cancel subscriptions through the appropriate platform before requesting account deletion.

Examples include:

your app marketplace
Apple App Store
Subscription management is controlled by the platform provider.
