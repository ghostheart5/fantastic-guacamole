# Axiomara Roadmap

The roadmap follows one product test: **does this make a real decision more informed, explainable, and controllable?**

## Now — connected intelligence

- Strengthen Planner context grounding and follow-up continuity.
- Make SI entity filters, scenarios, horizons, and Advanced mode visibly affect analysis.
- Keep Energy, Clarity, and Momentum consistent across Nexus and Trajectory.
- Improve failure responses so missing evidence produces a precise recovery step.
- Continue accessibility, resilience, privacy, and release validation.

## Next — deeper decision modeling

- Richer comparisons between competing goals and schedule constraints.
- User-visible provenance for the records behind a recommendation.
- Better scenario branching and side-by-side trade-off review.
- Clearer progression checkpoints and leverage explanations.
- Stronger user controls for conversational memory and attached context.

## Horizon — adaptive personal operations

- Long-range planning across changing capacity and priorities.
- Cross-surface decision threads that remain inspectable.
- Simulations that distinguish forecast, assumption, and user commitment.
- Personalized guidance that learns from explicit feedback without hiding why it changed.

Roadmap items describe direction, not a guaranteed delivery date. Safety, evidence quality, and user control remain release gates.
