# Core concepts

> Guide updated October 23, 2026. Screen names can vary by installed version. The October 22–23 repairs are tested and merged; this does not establish that a new Google Play build is available. See [[Release Notes|Release-Notes]].

**Records:** tasks, goals, habits and notes describe what matters to you. Save deliberately and keep statuses accurate.

**Planning:** My plan reviews those records; Schedule places commitments in time. A planned start and a hard deadline have different meanings.

**Action and progress:** completing real work produces recorded activity. XP and levels are feedback, not money or a measure of personal worth.

**Comparison:** Predictions creates a modeled preview from selected inputs. Complete, Delay and Remove here do not edit the real record. Change the draft, then compare again.

**Guidance:** AI/SI Chat uses the context you choose. Review evidence, assumptions, consent and any displayed credit quote. Suggestions do not silently authorize saved changes.

**Control:** Settings manages preferences, guidance, reminders, credits and privacy. Backups have a defined scope; do not assume every type of record is covered.
