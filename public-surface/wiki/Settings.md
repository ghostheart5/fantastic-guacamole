# Settings

> Guide updated October 23, 2026. Screen names can vary by installed version. The October 22–23 repairs are tested and merged; this does not establish that a new Google Play build is available. See [[Release Notes|Release-Notes]].

Use Settings to manage account preferences, appearance and language, planning and guidance, reminders, privacy, support and credit information. Some sections expand to show their controls.

## Guidance without losing your work

**Show the guide again** explains: “Shows the guide again without changing your saved work or activity.” **Welcome and account setup** explains: “Reopens welcome and account setup. Keeps your saved work and guide progress.” Review these as separate actions. Reopening guidance is not a request to erase records.

## Free tools and AI credits

Core planning and free tools are separate from quoted cloud questions. Review the price before confirming a cloud request. Eligible accounts may receive 20 included monthly credits without rollover; this is not a grant to every account or unlimited AI. Check your own balance, allowance and reset information in Settings. Purchased credits and included credits are distinct.

## Reminders

Check whether Reflection Reminder or Goal Reminders is enabled and verify the displayed time. Android notification permission, battery rules and device state can affect delivery. One reviewed notification arrived about 102 seconds after its temporary target; exact-time delivery is not guaranteed. A notification is a prompt, not proof of completion.

## Account, privacy and recovery

Review consent and selected context before external AI use. Sign-out, account deletion and store subscription cancellation are different actions. Deleting the app or account does not cancel store billing. Optional Cloud Backup covers tasks, profile information and account preferences; goals, habits, notes and history are excluded. Keep the recovery key separately and read the current recovery instructions before restoring.
